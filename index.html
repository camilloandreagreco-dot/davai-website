FAQ

Objections? Handled.

Is Dav.ai a CRM?

No. It's where the calling happens, on top of your CRM. Bring lists from any CRM export, a CSV or a plain paste.

Do I have to drop Sales Navigator, Apollo or ZoomInfo?

No. Keep them and import their exports. On a calling day the Agent finds the people and the numbers inside Dav.ai, with Lusha built in, so those tabs can stay closed.

Does the Agent make the calls?

No. You do, from your own phone, in one click. People buy from people. The Agent does everything around the call.

Can it send something I didn't approve?

No. Every email and invite waits for you. Press play to send it, reshape it by voice, or edit it by hand.

Will it make things up?

It's built not to. Drafts use only what you said and the documents you gave it, never invented facts, prices or dates.

Does it record my prospects?

Dav.ai is built to transcribe your side of the call, not the prospect's. You can also just say what happened after you hang up.

Where do the phone numbers come from?

Lusha, built in. The Agent shows you the plan first and spends no credits until you say go. Names and titles are free.

How much time will we really save?

It depends on your process. Our estimate for one call that books a meeting: about 16 minutes of switching and admin without Dav.ai, under a minute with it. Try the calculator with your own numbers.

Do I need IT to start?

No. Connect nothing and start calling the same day. Connect a mailbox and calendar later if you want to.

What exactly is the 5× guarantee?

Five times more outbound activity, or your money back. The full terms come with your quote.
